

CONTEXT

QUESTIONS

Translate each English sentence into Lezgian.

**Q1.** The children (pl) give (to) the dogs (pl).

**Q2.** The man (sg) was eating the dogs' (pl) water (sg) (I heard).

**Q3.** Do the dogs (pl) not eat the houses' (pl) water (sg)?

**Q4.** The children's (pl) dogs (pl) ate the waters' (pl) road (sg) (I heard).

**Q5.** Are the children (pl) going on the roads (pl)?

**Q6.** Do the children's (pl) dogs (pl) not see the houses' (pl) water (sg)?

ANSWERS

**Q1.** dal-il bat-id dʷa.

**Q2.** kər-il bat-in tara vur-t-r.

**Q3.** bat-il kora-a-n tara vur-mq?

**Q4.** dal-in bat-il tara-a-n zera vur-t-r.

**Q5.** zera-a-s dal-i tʼa-a-q?

**Q6.** dal-in bat-il kora-a-n tara kʷa-mq?

SOLUTION

**Inferred rules:**

**1. Noun stems and plural allomorphy.**
Stems divide into vowel-final (tara, kora, zera) and consonant-final (bat, kər, dal). Plural is marked by -a after vowel-final stems (sentences 2: tara-a) and -i after consonant-final stems (sentence 3: bat-i).

**2. Case marking.**
Four cases are marked by suffixes. The ergative is -il with no allomorphy (attested on both V-final and C-final stems: sent. 9: zera-a-il; sent. 5: kər-il; sent. 11/14: bat-il). The genitive, dative, and locative show two-way allomorphy conditioned by the stem's final segment:

| Case | After V-final | After C-final |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| Ergative | -il | -il |
| Genitive | -n | -in |
| Dative | -d | -id |
| Locative | -s | -is |

Evidence: ergative -il (sent. 5: kər-il; sent. 9: zera-a-il); genitive -n (sent. 6: tara-n) vs. -in (sent. 15: kər-in); dative -d (sent. 7: kora-d) vs. -id (sent. 13: dal-id); locative -s (sent. 8: zera-s).

**3. Plural + case on vowel-final stems (no deletion).**
The plural suffix -a simply precedes the case suffix: zera-a-il (sent. 9), tara-a-n (sent. 10).

**4. Plural + case on consonant-final stems (deletion of -i).**
The plural suffix -i is deleted before any case suffix. Evidence:
- Sent. 11: bat-il = "dogs (pl) eat" — the form is identical to singular ergative (sent. 14: bat-il = "dog (sg) eats"), but the translation confirms plurality. Underlying: bat-i-il → bat-il.
- Sent. 12: kər-in = "men's (pl)" — identical to singular genitive (sent. 15: kər-in = "man's (sg)"). Underlying: kər-i-in → kər-in.
- Sent. 13: dal-id = "children (pl)" (dative) — identical to what singular dative would be. Underlying: dal-i-id → dal-id.

This deletion applies before all case suffixes (erg, gen, dat, loc).

**5. Ergative-absolutive alignment.**
In transitive clauses, the agent bears ergative case and the patient is unmarked (absolutive). The verb carries no person/number agreement. Evidence: sent. 5 (kər-il tara vur: man-erg water-abs eat), sent. 9 (zera-a-il bat-i kʷa: roads-erg dogs-abs see).

**6. Modifier-before-noun order.**
Genitive possessors precede the head noun (sent. 6: tara-n kora; sent. 10: tara-a-n kora; sent. 24: kora-a-n tara).

**7. Intransitive clause word order: Adv-loc + S-abs + V.**
In intransitive clauses with a locative adverbial, the locative NP precedes the subject, which precedes the verb. Evidence: sent. 8 (zera-s kər tʼa: road-loc man-abs go).

**8. Verbal morphology: suffix order and allomorphy.**
Suffix order: Stem + Aspect + Tense + Evidentiality + Negation + Interrogative.

| Category | Marked | Unmarked |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Aspect | imperfective: -a | perfective: ∅ |
| Tense | past: -t | non-past: ∅ |
| Evidentiality | indirect: -r | direct: ∅ |
| Negation | negative: -m | affirmative: ∅ |
| Interrogative | question: -q | statement: ∅ |

Evidence: perfective non-past = bare stem (sent. 5: vur); past = -t (sent. 16: vur-t); imperfective = -a (sent. 17: vur-a); indirect = -r (sent. 19: vur-t-r); negation = -m (sent. 20: vur-m); interrogative = -q (sent. 21: vur-q).

**9. Aspect-tense deletion (imperfective -a deleted before past -t).**
Sent. 18: vur-t = "was eating" (imperfective past). Underlying: vur-a-t → vur-t. This is surface-identical to perfective past (sent. 16: vur-t = "ate"). The distinction is only recoverable from semantic context.

**10. Evidentiality conditioned on past tense.**
The suffix -r (indirect evidentiality) appears only in the presence of -t (past). It never appears on non-past verbs in the data. Evidence: sent. 19 (vur-t-r: past + indirect) vs. sent. 16 (vur-t: past + direct). The semantic contrast ("I heard" vs. no evidential marking) confirms -r encodes source of information, not tense.

**11. Negation-interrogative fusion.**
In negated questions, -m and -q fuse into a single surface form -mq. Evidence: sent. 22 (vur-mq? = "Does... not...?"). The form -m-q never appears; only -mq is attested.

**Answers derived:**

**Q1.** dal-il bat-id dʷa.
- dal (C-final) + i (pl) + il (erg) → dal-il (deletion of -i)
- bat (C-final) + i (pl) + id (dat) → bat-id (deletion of -i)
- dʷa (give, perfective non-past)
- Order: A-erg + O-dat + V

**Q2.** kər-il bat-in tara vur-t-r.
- kər-il: C-final sg ergative
- bat-in: bat (C-final) + i (pl) + in (gen) → bat-in (deletion)
- tara: V-final sg absolutive
- vur-t-r: vur + a (imperf) + t (past) → vur-t (deletion of -a) + r (indirect)
- Order: A-erg + [P-gen P-abs] + V

**Q3.** bat-il kora-a-n tara vur-mq?
- bat-il: bat (C-final) + i (pl) + il (erg) → bat-il (deletion)
- kora-a-n: kora (V-final) + a (pl) + n (gen) — no deletion
- tara: V-final sg absolutive
- vur-mq: vur + mq (negation-interrogative fusion)
- Order: A-erg + [P-gen P-abs] + V

**Q4.** dal-in bat-il tara-a-n zera vur-t-r.
- dal-in: dal (C-final) + i (pl) + in (gen) → dal-in (deletion)
- bat-il: bat (C-final) + i (pl) + il (erg) → bat-il (deletion)
- tara-a-n: tara (V-final) + a (pl) + n (gen) — no deletion
- zera: V-final sg absolutive
- vur-t-r: vur + a (imperf) + t (past) → vur-t (deletion) + r (indirect)
- Order: [A-gen A-erg] + [P-gen P-abs] + V

**Q5.** zera-a-s dal-i tʼa-a-q?
- zera-a-s: zera (V-final) + a (pl) + s (loc) — no deletion
- dal-i: dal (C-final) + i (pl) — absolutive, no case suffix, so NO deletion
- tʼa-a-q: tʼa + a (imperf) + q (interrogative) — NO deletion of -a (no -t present); NO fusion (affirmative question)
- Order: Adv-loc + S-abs + V (per sent. 8)

**Q6.** dal-in bat-il kora-a-n tara kʷa-mq?
- dal-in: dal (C-final) + i (pl) + in (gen) → dal-in (deletion)
- bat-il: bat (C-final) + i (pl) + il (erg) → bat-il (deletion)
- kora-a-n: kora (V-final) + a (pl) + n (gen) — no deletion
- tara: V-final sg absolutive
- kʷa-mq: kʷa + mq (negation-interrogative fusion)
- Order: [A-gen A-erg] + [P-gen P-abs] + V

DIFFICULTY

**Level 5.** The puzzle requires the solver to discover and compose multiple interacting rules: (a) a two-way allomorphy system for most nominal suffixes (genitive, dative, locative) plus a uniform ergative; (b) a deletion rule creating genuine singular/plural ambiguity on consonant-final nouns; (c) a parallel deletion rule in the verbal system creating perfective/imperfective past ambiguity; (d) a conditioned rule (evidentiality requires past tense); (e) a fusion rule (negation + interrogative). The production questions require applying 3–5 rules simultaneously in configurations not present in the data, and Q5 specifically tests the solver's understanding of the *conditions* under which rules do NOT apply (no deletion without a case suffix; no -a deletion without -t; no fusion in affirmative questions) as well as the intransitive word order (Adv-loc + S-abs + V).
