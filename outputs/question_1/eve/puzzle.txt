

CONTEXT

Evenki is a Tungusic language spoken in northeastern China and the Russian Far East. Below are some Evenki sentences with English translations.

DATA

1. ne dara tara-a tun-s — The child goes to the big river.
2. sira tala kora-t kela-s — The man sees the old reindeer.
3. ana nara du-t mala-s — The mother eats the new meat.
4. ne bira-m-a tun-s — The child goes to my tent.
5. sira ne-r-t kela-s — The man sees your child.
6. ana bira-s-t amar-n kela-s — The mother sees Amar's tent.
7. tul kora-s-t ana-n mala-s — Tul eats the mother's reindeer.
8. ne tara-a tun-s — The child goes to the river.
9. ne tara-a mura tun-e-s — The child does not go to the river.
10. sira kora-t kela-s — The man sees the reindeer.
11. sira kora-t mura kela-e-s — The man does not see the reindeer.
12. ne dara bira-s-t amar-n mura kela-e-s — The child does not see Amar's big tent.
13. sira tala bira-i nala-s — The man falls at the old tent.
14. tul tula kora-t mura kela-e-s — Tul does not see the white reindeer.

QUESTIONS

Translate between Evenki and English.

A. ne nara tara-a tun-s

B. tul nara bira-r-a tun-s

C. ana tala du-t mura mala-e-s

D. sira dara kora-s-t tul-n mura kela-e-s

E. The child sees Amar's old tent.

F. The mother does not see your big reindeer.

G. Tul goes to the man's new tent.

H. The child does not eat the mother's new meat.

ANSWERS

A. The child goes to the new river.
B. Tul goes to your new tent.
C. The mother does not eat the old meat.
D. The man does not see Tul's big reindeer.
E. ne tala bira-s-t amar-n kela-s
F. ana dara kora-r-t mura kela-e-s
G. tul nara bira-s-a sira-n tun-s
H. ne nara du-s-t ana-n mura mala-e-s

SOLUTION

**Inferred rules:**

1. **Word order:** SOV (Subject–Object–Verb). The negative word *mura* appears immediately before the verb.

2. **Modifier order:** Adjectives precede the noun they modify (e.g., *dara tara* "big river," *tala kora* "old reindeer").

3. **Case suffixes** (on nouns, after any possessive suffix):
   - Nominative: ∅ (unmarked)
   - Genitive: *-n* (marks the possessor in a possessive phrase)
   - Accusative: *-t* (marks the direct object)
   - Locative: *-i* (marks location)
   - Allative: *-a* (marks goal/destination)

4. **Possessive suffixes** (on the possessed noun, before any case suffix):
   - 1sg: *-m*
   - 2sg: *-r*
   - 3sg: *-s*
   - 3pl: *-w*

   The possessive suffix encodes the person/number of the possessor. For 1sg and 2sg, the possessor is a zero pronoun (understood from the suffix). For 3sg, the possessor appears as an explicit noun with the genitive suffix *-n*.

5. **Double marking in possession:** Both elements in a possessive phrase carry overt marking — the possessor receives a genitive case suffix, and the possessed noun receives a possessive suffix agreeing with the possessor.

6. **Person/number agreement on verbs:**
   - 1sg: *-m*, 2sg: *-r*, 3sg: *-s*, 1pl: *-l*, 2pl: *-k*, 3pl: *-w*

7. **Double negation:** Negation requires two elements:
   - The preverbal negative word *mura* (immediately before the verb)
   - The negative suffix *-e* on the verb (between the stem and the agreement suffix)

   Neither element alone is sufficient; both must co-occur.

8. **Suffix order:**
   - On nouns: stem + possessive suffix + case suffix
   - On verbs: stem + negative suffix + agreement suffix

**Solutions to questions:**

A. *ne* (child, subject) + *nara* (new, modifier) + *tara-a* (river-allative) + *tun-s* (go-3sg) → "The child goes to the new river."

B. *tul* (Tul, subject) + *nara* (new, modifier) + *bira-r-a* (tent-2sg.poss-allative) + *tun-s* (go-3sg) → "Tul goes to your new tent."

C. *ana* (mother, subject) + *tala* (old, modifier) + *du-t* (meat-acc) + *mura* (NEG) + *mala-e-s* (eat-NEG-3sg) → "The mother does not eat the old meat."

D. *sira* (man, subject) + *dara* (big, modifier) + *kora-s-t* (reindeer-3sg.poss-acc) + *tul-n* (Tul-Gen) + *mura* (NEG) + *kela-e-s* (see-NEG-3sg) → "The man does not see Tul's big reindeer."

E. "The child sees Amar's old tent." → *ne* (child) + *tala* (old, before noun) + *bira-s-t* (tent-3sg.poss-acc) + *amar-n* (Amar-Gen) + *kela-s* (see-3sg) → **ne tala bira-s-t amar-n kela-s**

F. "The mother does not see your big reindeer." → *ana* (mother) + *dara* (big, before noun) + *kora-r-t* (reindeer-2sg.poss-acc) + *mura* (NEG) + *kela-e-s* (see-NEG-3sg) → **ana dara kora-r-t mura kela-e-s**

G. "Tul goes to the man's new tent." → *tul* (Tul) + *nara* (new, before noun) + *bira-s-a* (tent-3sg.poss-allative) + *sira-n* (man-Gen) + *tun-s* (go-3sg) → **tul nara bira-s-a sira-n tun-s**

H. "The child does not eat the mother's new meat." → *ne* (child) + *nara* (new, before noun) + *du-s-t* (meat-3sg.poss-acc) + *ana-n* (mother-Gen) + *mura* (NEG) + *mala-e-s* (eat-NEG-3sg) → **ne nara du-s-t ana-n mura mala-e-s**
