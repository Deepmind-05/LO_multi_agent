## QUESTIONS

## DIFFICULTY

**Rating: 4/10**

The solver must identify five morphological elements (the have-verb *tá*, the negative suffix *-kú*, the question suffix *-mí*, the possessive prefixes *wa-/ya-/ta-*, and the genitive suffix *-ne*) and recognize that two strategies (full-NP subject vs. possessive marking) are both valid. Each question is a straightforward application of these rules with clear parallel examples in the data.

## SOLUTION / RATIONALE

**Rules inferred from the data:**

1. **Have-verb (SOV):** *tá* is a verb meaning "have." The possessor is the subject (first position), the possessed noun is the object (second position), and the verb is final. The verb does not mark person.

2. **Negative suffix:** *-kú* attaches to the verb to form negation. Evidenced by the contrast between *masá kóla tá.* and *masá kóla tá-kú.*

3. **Question suffix:** *-mí* attaches to the verb to form polar questions. Word order is unchanged. Evidenced by the contrast between *yasá péna tá.* and *yasá kóla tá-mí?*

4. **Genitive suffix:** *-ne* attaches to a full-NP possessor. E.g. *Anil-ne kóla tá.* = "Anil has a book."

5. **Possessive prefixes:** When the possessor is a pronoun, it can be encoded as a prefix on the possessed noun: *wa-* (1sg), *ya-* (2sg), *ta-* (3sg). E.g. *wakóla tá.* = "I have a book."

6. **Two valid strategies:** Both the full-NP subject + have-verb construction and the possessive-marking + have-verb construction are valid. Evidenced by the parallel pairs: *masá kóla tá.* = *wakóla tá.* (both "I have a book"); *yasá péna tá.* = *yapéna tá.* (both "You have a house"); *nasá sáru tá.* = *tasáru tá.* (both "He/She has money").

**Application to questions:**

- (a) 1sg, negative → both *masá tíma tá-kú.* and *watíma tá-kú.*
- (b) Full NP, question → both *Gita kóla tá-mí?* and *Gita-ne kóla tá-mí?*
- (c) 2sg → both *yasá tíma tá.* and *yatíma tá.*
- (d) 1sg → both *masá sáru tá.* and *wasáru tá.*
- (e) 3sg, negative → both *nasá kóla tá-kú.* and *takóla tá-kú.*
- (f) Full NP, question → both *Bindu tíma tá-mí?* and *Bindu-ne tíma tá-mí?*
