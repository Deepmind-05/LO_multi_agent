**CONTEXT**  
Occitan (ISO 639‑3 occ) expresses possession with the verb **aver** “to have”.  
The possessor can be marked in two equivalent ways:

1. **Clitic pronoun attached to the verb** – e.g. *l’ai* “I have it”.  
2. **Prepositional phrase *a + NP*** – e.g. *ai lo libre a Joan* “I have the book at Joan”.

A genitive construction with **de** (*de + NP* before the noun) is used **only when the possessor is animate (a person or a kinship noun)**.  With inanimate possessors the *a + NP* construction is required.  

Negation is expressed by the particle **pas** placed after the verb (*ai pas*).  
Polar (yes‑no) questions are formed by intonation only; the written form is identical to the statement, differing only by a trailing “?”.  

Cardinal numerals agree in gender (and number) with the noun they modify:

* **un** masc., **una** fem. – “one”  
* **dos** masc., **daus** fem. – “two”

Mass nouns (e.g. *aiga* “water”) never take a numeral.  

The verb **aver** shows number agreement with its subject: *ai* (1st sg.), *avèm* (1st pl.).

**SOLUTION / RATIONALE**

1. **Possessor marking** – Possession is expressed with *aver* plus the possessed noun. Two equivalent strategies are attested:  
   - a clitic pronoun attached to the verb (*l’ai*).  
   - a prepositional phrase *a + NP* (*ai … a Joan*).  
   Both appear in the data (sentences 1‑3, 7, 20, 22).

2. **Verb agreement** – *aver* agrees in number with its subject: *ai* (1st sg.), *avèm* (1st pl.). Evident in sentences 1, 4, 21.

3. **Negation** – The particle **pas** follows the verb and negates the whole predicate (sentences 5, 6, 12, 17). It can co‑occur with either possessor‑marking variant (Q2b).

4. **Polar questions** – No lexical change; a trailing “?” signals a question (sentences 7, 18, 22). Thus only the presence of “?” determines question status (Q6).

5. **Genitive with *de*** – The preposition *de* marks a genitive relationship **only when the possessor is animate** (a person or a kinship noun). This is supported by:  
   - *Lo libre de Joan* (sentence 13) – animate possessor (person).  
   - *La maire de Joan* (sentence 15) – kinship noun.  
   The construction is ungrammatical with an inanimate possessor, e.g. *Lo clau de la casa* (sentence iii of Q3) is ruled out.

6. **Gender‑agreeing numerals** – Numerals *un/una* and *dos/daus* agree in gender with the noun they modify (sentences 8‑11). Mass nouns never take a numeral (sentence 16). The pattern extends to other numerals (e.g. *tres* for “three”).

7. **Possessive adjectives for own kinship nouns** – When the possessor is the speaker, kinship nouns take a possessive adjective (*ma maire*) rather than the *de*‑genitive (sentence 14). Hence Q7’s answer.

All questions can be answered by applying these rules to the supplied data; no external knowledge is required.
