SOLUTION

**Hidden rules:**

1. **Word order:** SOV. The predicate (verb or predicative adjective) is always the final element of the clause (before any question particle).

2. **Case marking:**
   - Nominative (subject): zero (unmarked)
   - Accusative (object): -ta
   - Locative (complement of a postposition): -na
   - Genitive (possessor): -ka

3. **Negation:** Two components:
   - An obligatory suffix -ne attached to the predicate (appears in every negative sentence).
   - An optional independent word "kora" that may appear immediately after the predicate (appears in some but not all negative sentences).

4. **Predicative adjectives:** In predicative position ("X is [adj]"), the adjective occupies the verb position and takes the negative suffix -ne in negative sentences. It behaves identically to a verb in all syntactic respects. In attributive position, it precedes the noun it modifies.

5. **Postpositions:** "tara" (in/inside) and "hura" (on/above) follow a locative-marked noun (-na). The postpositional phrase appears in pre-verbal position.

6. **Question particle:** "ma" appears at the absolute end of the clause to mark polar questions. In negative questions, it follows the optional negative word.

7. **No verbal agreement:** The verb form is invariant with respect to person and number of arguments.

**Q1 reasoning:**
The subject is the unmarked noun (nominative); the object bears -ta (accusative). In (d), there is only one NP and no -ta, so the sentence is intransitive.

**Q2 reasoning:**
The presence of -ne on the predicate marks negation. The word "kora" after the predicate is the optional negative word. In (c), there is no -ne, so the sentence is positive.

**Q3 reasoning:**
Examining all negative sentences (7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15, 16, 20), the suffix -ne is attached to the predicate in every one of them. This is the obligatory negative marker.

**Q4 reasoning:**
a. S = nura (unmarked), O = sura-ta (accusative), V = tana → nura sura-ta tana
b. S = sura (unmarked), O = nura-ta (accusative), V = dara-ne (negative) → sura nura-ta dara-ne
c. S = mala (unmarked), O = taro-ta (accusative), V = tana, Q = ma → mala taro-ta tana ma
d. Possessor = taro-ka (genitive), Possessed = pala, Adj = sila → taro-ka pala sila
