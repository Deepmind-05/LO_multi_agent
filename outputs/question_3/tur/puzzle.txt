SOLUTION

**Rules to be inferred:**

**R1 – Possessive suffixes (dependent marking).** The possessed noun carries a suffix encoding the person and number of the possessor. Three persons are attested:
- 1sg: -ım / -im / -um / -üm
- 2sg: -ın / -in / -un / -ün
- 3sg: -ı / -i / -u / -ü

Evidence: Items 1–15 show the same stems with three different suffix sets corresponding to "my," "your," and "his/her." The 1sg and 2sg forms contain an additional /n/ compared to 3sg.

**R2 – Vowel harmony (4-way).** The vowel of every suffix is determined by the last vowel of the stem it attaches to, along two dimensions:
- Frontness: front stem vowel (/e/, /i/, /ö/, /ü/) → front suffix vowel; back stem vowel (/a/, /ı/, /o/, /u/) → back suffix vowel.
- Roundedness: rounded stem vowel (/o/, /u/, /ö/, /ü/) → rounded suffix vowel; unrounded stem vowel (/a/, /ı/, /e/, /i/) → unrounded suffix vowel.

The specific suffix vowel matches the stem's last vowel: /a/→/a/, /ı/→/ı/, /e/→/e/, /i/→/i/, /o/→/o/, /u/→/u/, /ö/→/ö/, /ü/→/ü/.

Evidence: Comparing items 1 (ev→-im), 4 (kapı→-ım), 7 (göz→-üm), 10 (kutu→-um) isolates both dimensions. Items 13–15 (kitap, ending in /a/) show the same suffix forms as items 4–6 (kapı, ending in /ı/), confirming that /a/ and /ı/ belong to the same harmony class (back unrounded).

**R3 – Genitive case (full-NP possessor).** When the possessor is a full noun phrase (not a pronoun), it receives a genitive suffix: -ın / -in / -un / -ün (harmonizing to the possessor's stem).

Evidence: Items 16–19 show a noun followed by a suffix, then a possessed noun with a 3sg possessive suffix. The glosses identify the first noun as the possessor.

**R4 – Double marking.** In full-NP possession, BOTH the possessor receives genitive AND the possessed noun receives a 3sg possessive suffix. In pronominal possession, only the possessive suffix appears.

Evidence: Contrast item 1 (evim, "my house" — suffix only) with item 16 (Ali-nin evi, "Ali's house" — genitive + possessive suffix).

**R5 – Linear order: possessor before possessed.** The genitive-marked possessor precedes the possessed noun.

Evidence: Items 16–19 consistently show the genitive-marked noun first.

**R6 – Plural suffix.** The plural suffix is -lar (after back stems) / -ler (after front stems), harmonizing like all other suffixes.

Evidence: Item 23 (kapılar, "doors") shows the plural on a back stem; items 20–21 (evlerim, gözlerim) show it on front stems.

**R7 – Suffix stacking order.** When a noun carries multiple suffixes, the fixed order is: Root → Plural → Possessive → Genitive.

Evidence: Item 24 (evlerimin kapısı) shows ev + -ler + -im + -in (Root-PL-1SG.POSS-GEN), establishing Plural before Possessive and Possessive before Genitive. Items 26–28 confirm this order in nested constructions.

**R8 – Stem-local harmony.** In a multi-noun genitive chain, each suffix harmonizes to the stem it is directly attached to, not to a preceding stem.

Evidence: Item 27 (kedi-nin gözünün anahtarı): -in harmonizes to kedi (/i/, front unrounded), -ü and -nün harmonize to göz (/ö/, front rounded), -ı harmonizes to anahtar (/a/, back unrounded). Three different harmony classes in one chain.

**Answers:**

**Q1.1** "your books": stem kitap (/a/, back unrounded) + plural -lar + 2sg possessive -ın → **kitapların**

**Q1.2** "the cat's box": kedi (/i/, front unrounded) + genitive -in → kedi-nin; kutu (/u/, back rounded) + 3sg possessive -u → kutusu → **kedi-nin kutusu**

**Q1.3** "my boxes": kutu (/u/, back rounded) + plural -lar + 1sg possessive -ım → **kutularım**

**Q1.4** "the child's houses": çocuk (/u/, back rounded) + genitive -un → çocuk-nun; ev (/e/, front unrounded) + plural -ler + 3sg possessive -i → evleri → **çocuk-nun evleri**

**Q2.5** "the door of Fatma's box": Fatma (/a/, back unrounded) + genitive -ın → Fatma-nın; kutu (/u/, back rounded) + 3sg possessive -u + genitive -nun → kutusunun; kapı (/ı/, back unrounded) + 3sg possessive -ı → kapısı → **Fatma-nın kutusunun kapısı**

**Q2.6** "the keys of the doors of my houses": ev (/e/) + -ler + -im + -in → evlerimin; kapı (/ı/) + -lar + -ı + -nın → kapılarının; anahtar (/a/) + -lar → anahtarları → **evlerimin kapılarının anahtarları**

**Q2.7** "the door of the box of the child's house": çocuk (/u/) + -un → çocuk-nun; ev (/e/) + -i + -nin → evinin; kutu (/u/) + -u + -nun → kutusunun; kapı (/ı/) + -ı → kapısı → **çocuk-nun evinin kutusunun kapısı**

DIFFICULTY

**Level 2.** The puzzle requires the solver to discover four interacting systems (vowel harmony, possessive morphology, genitive construction, suffix ordering) from a flat list of glossed forms, then apply them in combination to produce novel multi-suffix chains. The difficulty arises from: (a) decomposing the 4-way harmony into two independent binary dimensions; (b) distinguishing the genitive from the formally identical 2sg possessive by position; (c) tracking stem-local harmony across three or four different stems in a single chain; (d) correctly applying the full suffix stack (Root-PL-POSS-GEN) in nested constructions. Each question in Q2 requires simultaneous application of at least four inferred rules.
